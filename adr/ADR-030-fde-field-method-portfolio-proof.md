# ADR-030: FDE Field Method as portfolio proof (not a sixth platform)

## Status
Accepted — 2026-07-31

## Context
Public positioning was Principal/Architect-first. FDE / Applied AI demand is high, but building another mega product would dilute the 5-spine review path and violate simplicity. The missing FDE signal is **method + embed narrative**, not another runtime.

## Decision
Ship an inspectable **FDE Field Method** (discovery → score → wedge → govern → eval → handoff) as:

1. Live page: `https://venkat-ai.com/fde`
2. Case study in this portfolio
3. Playbook track `fde-deployment/` + `release/fde_pack.json` (99 graded entries under the 100 cap)

Reuse AegisAI, VAP, Enterprise RAG, and Content Factory as the **deployed layers** under that method. Do **not** invent external customer logos; Lucid Supply Chain / Commerce remains an **internal customer embed**.

## Consequences
- Dual-fit brand (Architect + FDE) without two LinkedIn personas
- Panels get a week-1 discovery checklist they can grill
- Candidate must still practice voice mocks and coding; curriculum ≠ offer
- Risk: method page without timed practice reads as theater — mitigated by playbook Arena rubrics
